Answer:
The paper argues for the Transformer architecture, which relies entirely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions, due to several advantages. The three primary advantages of self-attention mentioned are:

1. **Parallelization**: The Transformer allows for significantly more parallelization compared to models that use recurrence, which leads to faster training times [Source 4].

2. **Modeling Dependencies**: Attention mechanisms enable the modeling of dependencies without regard to their distance in the input or output sequences, which is a fundamental constraint in sequential computation models [Source 2].

3. **Performance**: The Transformer achieves superior quality in tasks such as machine translation, as evidenced by its state-of-the-art BLEU scores on the WMT 2014 English-to-German and English-to-French translation tasks [Source 4].
